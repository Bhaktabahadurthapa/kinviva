# Website design notes

Scope: the public marketing site only (`site/`, generated by `scripts/gen-site.py`). Not the desktop app; `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md` describe the app and are not touched.

## Direction
An annotated transcript on warm paper. Navy ink, one blue marker, a serif voice for headlines, a sans voice for everything else. Evidence over decoration: real product screenshots, status tags with sources, and invented examples labelled as such.

## System
- **Colour:** midnight navy `#0B1020`, royal blue `#2563EB` (text links use `#1D4ED8` for contrast), violet `#7C3AED`, paper `#F4F0E6`, slate body `#2A3350`. Status colours: green, amber, violet (planned), grey dashed (not verified).
- **Type:** Fraunces (headlines, display up to 96px), Instrument Sans (body and labels), IBM Plex Mono only for transcript data and captions. All self-hosted; no third-party requests. Half-pixel sizes were folded into whole steps (12.5 to 13, 13.5 to 14, 14.5 to 15, 15.5 to 16).
- **Depth:** content plates use one soft shadow token (`--depth`). Buttons and the social keys use a small extruded edge, a deliberate 3D "key" style.
- **Layout:** 1200px container, 1320px from 1680px up. Menu collapses at 900px. Sections alternate paper and paper-2; variety comes from the content shape (ruled lists, numbered steps, side-by-side panels, comparison table, flow card), not repeated cards.
- **Motion:** one hero entrance and a highlight that draws in; scroll reveals; button press. All off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Cache:** CSS and JS links carry a content hash, so updates are never served stale.

## Small screens
Screenshots keep a 780px width and pan sideways so their text stays readable. The tour shows two screens and drops its bullet lists. The status checklist folds behind a visible summary.

## Rules held on every page
No prices. Planned features are labelled planned. Competitor claims come from the vendor's own pages, with links and a check date (`docs/COMPETITOR_RESEARCH.md`). The Early Access form stays disabled until the service is deployed.
