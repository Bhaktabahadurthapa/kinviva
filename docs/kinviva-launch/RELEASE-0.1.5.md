## MeetingLens is now Kinviva

Same app, new name. This is the first release under the Kinviva name and the biggest update so far.

### Important when you update from MeetingLens
- On the first start Kinviva **copies** your data (sessions, notes, settings) from the old MeetingLens folder. The old folder is left untouched.
- macOS treats Kinviva as a new app: you will be asked again for **Microphone** and **Screen & System Audio Recording**, and the Keychain may ask once to let Kinviva read your saved API keys (choose Always Allow).
- Small display preferences (theme, panel sizes, recent videos) start fresh.

### What's new
- A new look: Dashboard, navy side menu, light and dark themes.
- **Video Assist:** watch a video next to its explanations, with Previous/Next, a clean player and full-screen mode.
- **Live answers** as numbered steps in the order spoken, with a "Now" step, an Earlier list, and search across everything.
- **Answer level and length**, a "Remember it" line, and **My instructions** (about you, instructions for each screen, ready-made styles).
- **Notes** rebuilt: rich editor, checklists, tables, folders, pinned notes, tags, gallery view, Recently Deleted.
- **Presentation maker:** plan slides from your own information and save an editable .pptx.
- **Classroom:** "I'm lost", homework and exam announcements, flashcards, homework hints.
- **Interview:** the interviewer's question shown in a bar beside your answer.
- **Visuals & Diagrams:** charts from a pasted table, CSV export, one searchable library.
- **Technical Copilot:** architecture diagrams from the conversation, safe read-only troubleshooting.
- **Discover** refreshes itself every hour; session history has a Trash with restore.

### Removed
- My Learning (the goals and plans screen).
