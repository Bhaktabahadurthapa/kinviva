Straight answers.

Does Kinviva record my calls?

Not unless you turn on "Save audio recordings" in Settings. By default it transcribes and saves text, not audio.

Where are my API keys stored?

In your system keychain (macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager). They are never written to the database, settings files or logs.

Is there telemetry or an account?

No. There is no account and no analytics. The app only contacts the speech and answer services you configure, and Discover if you turn it on.

Can others see Kinviva during screen sharing?

You can ask your system to keep the app and overlay out of screen capture. This works with most tools but is not guaranteed, so check it before an important call.

What does Read my screen send?

A shrunken screenshot of your main display, with the last few things said, to your answer engine. It runs only when you ask, or when you turn on its automatic mode. The image is deleted right after it is read.

Do I need to tell people I'm using it?

Some places require everyone's consent to record or transcribe a conversation, and some interviewers or employers have rules about assistance. You are responsible for following them.
