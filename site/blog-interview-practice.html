Interview mode: practise and answer without inventing your experience

By the Kinviva team · 8 November 2026 · 4 min read

In short
  • Each question appears in a banner beside your answer.
  • Practise first or run a mock interview and get feedback.
  • It never invents your experience. Everything is a draft for you.
On this page
  1. During an interview
  2. Practise first
  3. What it will not do
  4. Skills behind the answers

Interviews are a conversation with a time limit. Interview mode gives you a place to see the question, think, and shape an answer in your own words.

During an interview

Press Start and each question the interviewer asks appears in a banner at the top, beside your answer area. Choose your role and category, and an answer style: Simple, Professional or Student. Kinviva drafts a spoken answer plus the reasoning behind it, which you can edit.

Practise first

  • Mock practice: get a question for your role, write your answer, then get feedback on correctness, missing points and clarity.
  • Job description analysis: paste a job description to see the skills they want, likely questions and what to prepare, compared with your own documents.
  • Practice notes: download questions, your answers and the feedback as Markdown, Word or text.

What it will not do

If a question is about your own past work and your notes and documents do not say, Kinviva does not guess. It shows how to frame the answer and asks one clarifying question. It never presents an invented qualification as yours, and nothing is typed into the interview for you.

Skills behind the answers

Four bundled coaching skills guide technical answers: cloud and DevOps, system design, AI engineering and coding. They were written for Kinviva and tell the AI what a good answer needs.

Be honest with employers. Use Kinviva within the rules of the interview and local law. Some employers do not allow assistance during an interview. Practice mode is always safe to use.
