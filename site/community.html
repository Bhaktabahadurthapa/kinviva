The Community Edition.

Everything you can download today. Free to use, local-first, with no account. Nothing in it is a trial.

Local transcription

A built-in speech engine on your computer. Or add Deepgram with your own key.

Your own AI

Connect a local model or any OpenAI-compatible service with your own key. Your keys stay in the system keychain.

Explanations and history

Plain-English explanations, replies, recaps, decisions, saved sessions, a Trash, and exports to Word, Markdown and text.

Interview and classroom

Practise and answer interview questions; lecture notes, study guides, flashcards and quizzes.

Technical basics

Safe read-only troubleshooting plans, an incident tracker, and architecture diagrams from the conversation.

Notes, whiteboard, visuals

Rich notes with folders and tags, an editable whiteboard, charts from your own numbers, and a presentation maker.

What stays free.

Everything aboveIt stays in the Community Edition. Paid editions will add new things; they will not take these away.
Your dataReading, exporting and deleting your own data is never paid.
PrivacyNo telemetry and no Kinviva account in the Community Edition. See exactly what is sent in each setup.
