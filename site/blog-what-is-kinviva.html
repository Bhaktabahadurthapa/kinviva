What is Kinviva, and who is it for?

By the Kinviva team · 8 November 2026 · 4 min read

In short
  • Kinviva listens to a meeting, call, interview or lecture on your computer.
  • It explains each point in plain words and helps you prepare a reply.
  • It is free for Mac and Windows, needs no account, and has no Kinviva server.

Most meeting tools tell you what was said. Kinviva is built to help while it is being said. You press Start, and it writes a live transcript of the call and of your own voice. As each point finishes, it explains it in simple words and, when someone asks a question, drafts an answer you can edit.

The problem it is built for

Conversations move faster than people can follow. A manager meets a term they do not know. A student loses the thread of a lecture. A candidate is asked something in an interview and needs a moment to think. A team lead leaves a call and cannot remember who agreed to what. Kinviva tries to help in each of these moments.

What it does today

  • Live transcript. Two sources are kept apart: Them (the audio your computer plays) and Me (your microphone).
  • Plain-English explanations. Each finished point is explained, with hard words listed.
  • Suggested replies. A short draft grounded in what was said and in your own notes and documents. It is shown to you only. Nothing is typed into the call.
  • Recaps, decisions and action items. Decisions and actions come with the quote that proves them.
  • Saved history. Sessions are saved on your computer, searchable, and can be exported to Word, Markdown or text.
  • Technical Copilot, interview practice, classroom tools and a presentation maker for specific situations. Later posts explain each.

Who it is for

Anyone who has to follow fast conversations and answer well: people at work, engineers in technical calls, job candidates, and students. It is not built for large teams yet. There are no shared workspaces and no integrations with tools such as Slack or a CRM.

What you need

A Mac with Apple silicon (macOS 13 or later), or a Windows 10 or 11 PC. The Windows version is experimental and has not been tested on real hardware by us. You also need an answer engine: either your own key for a cloud AI service, or a free local model. The first time you start, your computer asks for microphone and system-audio permission.

What it does not do

  • It does not join your call as a bot, and it does not start by itself. You press Start.
  • Its answers come from the AI you connect and can be wrong. You decide what to say.
  • It is built for English today.
  • Pro and Business editions, including AI agents that complete tasks, are planned. They are not available.

Try it. Kinviva is free for Mac and Windows, with no account.
