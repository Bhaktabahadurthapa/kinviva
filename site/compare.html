Kinviva and other AI meeting tools.

Several tools do real-time help and action items well. This page shows what each vendor says it offers, where Kinviva is different, and where it is not ahead.

Typical meeting-notes workflow

  1. Capture and transcribe the discussion
  2. Generate a summary
  3. Review and search the notes
  4. Identify follow-up items

Kinviva's approach

  1. Follow the discussion as it happens
  2. Explain complex information
  3. Prepare context-aware responses
  4. Provide specialised technical guidance
  5. Support actionable follow-through

Kinviva may suit you if

  • You want explanations and reply drafts while the conversation is happening
  • You need the work to stay on your computer, with a local model option
  • Your conversations are technical, or you learn from lectures and videos
  • You prefer no account and no Kinviva server

Another tool may suit you better if

  • You want meetings captured automatically from your calendar
  • Your team needs shared workspaces and integrations such as Slack or a CRM
  • You need a vendor with an independent security certification today. Kinviva has none yet
  • You want mobile apps. Kinviva is a Mac and Windows desktop app, with Windows still experimental
